## Turkish valid-guess list (hunspell-tr)

- File: `shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/files/words/tr_valid.txt`
- Source: hunspell-tr by the Turkish Data Depository (TDD) group, <https://github.com/tdd-ai/hunspell-tr>.
  Contributors: Ali Safaya, Arda Göktoğan, Deniz Yuret, Emirhan Kurtuluş, Taner Sezer. The exact commit is
  written in the header of the file.
- License: Mozilla Public License 2.0, full text in [third_party/hunspell-tr/LICENSE](third_party/hunspell-tr/LICENSE).
- What we did: `tools/wordlists/build_tr.py` expands the dictionary's stems with their suffix flags and keeps only
  the five-letter forms written in the Turkish alphabet. The dictionary entries themselves are not edited. Because
  `tr_valid.txt` is derived from the MPL-licensed files, it stays under MPL-2.0 (not MIT). It is published in full in
  this repository, which is the Source Code Form, and the script above reproduces it from the upstream files.
- The Turkish answer pool (`tr_answers.txt`) is BrainScroll's own hand-reviewed selection
  (`tools/wordlists/tr_answers_curated.txt`, MIT) and is not covered by this notice.
