Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how the BrainScroll app and the playbrainscroll.com website handle your personal data. In short: we collect only what we need to run the app, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you for advertising.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Mehmetcan Kılınç (individual developer). Contact: support@playbrainscroll.com

2. What data we collect

Account data

  • When you first open the app, we create a random anonymous account ID.
  • If you choose to link your account with Google or Apple, we receive the account identifier from that provider and, if you choose to share it, your email address. We use this to recognize your account and carry it across devices.

Gameplay data

  • Your results, times, guesses, streaks, statistics, favorites and in-progress games.

Profile and social data

  • The username you choose and the friend connections you make with invite codes.
  • Your leaderboard entries. Your username and your scores, times and streaks are visible to other players.

Anonymous usage counts and crash reports

  • The app sends anonymous counts, such as which screens are opened and whether puzzles are started and finished (with the outcome and the number of guesses), and the error text when the app crashes.
  • These records contain only the event name, the platform (Android, iOS, desktop), the app version and a few small values. No account ID, device ID, advertising identifier or IP address is written into them, and they cannot be linked to you or to each other.
  • We delete these records after 90 days. You can turn sending off at any time in the "Help improve BrainScroll" section of the Profile screen; turning it off also deletes anything still waiting to be sent.

Technical data

  • Our backend provider may process your IP address and request information for security and abuse prevention, such as rate limiting.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier. We don't show third-party ads.

3. Why we use your data

  • To run the app: storing your account, streaks, statistics and favorites.
  • To provide leaderboards and friend features.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent cheating and abuse.
  • To understand which parts of the app are used and where it fails, so we can improve it (anonymous counts and crash reports).
  • To answer support requests and meet legal obligations.

Legal bases: performance of a contract (providing the service to you), our legitimate interests such as security and abuse prevention, and legal obligations (under GDPR Art. 6 and the Turkish KVKK Art. 5).

4. Where data is stored and who receives it

  • Supabase: our database and authentication provider. Your data is stored in the European Union, in the Frankfurt (Germany) region.
  • Apple and Google: only if you choose to sign in with them, under their own privacy policies.
  • Cloudflare: hosts our website and may keep visitor IP addresses in its own logs. The website uses no cookies or analytics tools.
  • Other players: only your username and scores, as shown on leaderboards and friend lists.

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with other people or organizations unless the law requires it.

Providers such as Apple and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area. That processing is governed by the providers' own policies.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account from the app with the "Delete account" button on the Profile screen (everything stored in the cloud and the data on the device is deleted right away), or by writing to support@playbrainscroll.com. After a deletion request we delete your data within a reasonable time. Copies in backups drop out on a short cycle.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to its processing and to take it with you. Send your request to support@playbrainscroll.com. You also have the right to complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK), or under the GDPR to the data protection authority in your country.

7. Children

BrainScroll is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no system is completely free of risk.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post significant changes on this page and change the "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

For questions and requests: support@playbrainscroll.com
