Frequently asked

Is it free?

Yes, every game is free. Hint packs and BrainScroll Pro are optional. We don't show third-party ads.

Do I need an account?

No. You play as soon as you open the app. You can link your account with Google or Apple to carry your progress across devices and to appear on leaderboards.

Is BrainScroll Pro a subscription?

No. It's a one-time payment: it doesn't renew and there is nothing to cancel.

Which devices?

We are building it for iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play). iPhone comes first.

What happens to my data?

We collect only what we need to run the app, and we never sell it. Details are in the privacy policy.
