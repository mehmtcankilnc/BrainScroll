Delete your account and data

Last updated: November 25, 2026

You can delete your BrainScroll account at any time. A deleted account cannot be restored. There are two ways: in the app (immediately) or by email.

1. Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open BrainScroll and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm Delete permanently in the window that opens.

Everything stored in the cloud is deleted right away, and so is the data on the device.

2. If you can't open the app, delete by email

If you have removed the app or can't reach your phone, write to support@playbrainscroll.com with the subject "Account deletion request". If you linked your account with Google or Apple, write from the email address of that account, so we can confirm the request comes from you. We carry out the request within a reasonable time, at most 30 days, and let you know.

If you never linked your account with Google or Apple (a guest account), the account is not tied to an email address and we can't confirm by email that it is yours. To delete a guest account, install the app and follow way 1; if you never installed the app, there is nothing in the account that identifies you.

What is deleted

  • Your account and session.
  • Your results, times, streaks, statistics, favorites and in-progress games.
  • Your username, leaderboard entries and friend connections.
  • The hint balance on your account and the records of the hint packs you bought. Hints you bought but have not used are lost at this point.

What is not deleted

  • Your store records. The record of your BrainScroll Pro and hint pack purchases stays with Apple or Google in your own account; we can't delete it. BrainScroll Pro is tied to the Apple or Google account you bought it with, so even after deleting your account you can restore it with the same store account. Refund requests go to Apple or Google.
  • Anonymous usage counts. These are counts that cannot be linked to your account, your device or you, and are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Backups. Copies in backups drop out on a short cycle.

How your data is handled is explained in the privacy policy.
